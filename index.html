Tiny Town Sim Uploader: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Tiny Town Sim Uploader is a small private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Tiny Town Sim. It uploads that channel's own videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to YouTube and schedules when they go live.

What the tool accesses

  • Permission to upload videos to the Tiny Town Sim channel (YouTube Data API, upload scope).
  • Read-only access to that channel's own video details, to check that each upload worked (title, visibility, processing status).

What the tool does not do

  • It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any viewer or any other person.
  • It does not read comments, messages, subscribers or watch history.
  • It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Where data is kept

The authorization token Google issues is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, so the tool can upload without asking again. It is never sent anywhere except to Google. The owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, its user is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact options on the Tiny Town Sim channel's About page on YouTube.
